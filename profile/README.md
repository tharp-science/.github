# THARP | Thyke Applied Research & Prototyping

Two people can look at the same evidence and reach different conclusions. THARP studies what happens when people and AI produce knowledge together: how findings emerge, how interpretations diverge, and what gets lost when systems turn them into a single answer.

To investigate these questions, we develop and test methods and instruments that make these processes visible, preserve their provenance, and keep conclusions open to challenge.

THARP is a not-for-profit applied research programme run by [Thyke i/c/w Commons](https://thyke-commons.eu).

| **Working prototype** | **Context Mediation Layer** — a shared record connecting intent, interpretation, authority, evidence and action across people and AI.
